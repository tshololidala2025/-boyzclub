WHY IT MATTERS

Boys need safe spaces, consistent guidance and positive examples too.

Boyzclub is designed to complement existing youth support by creating intentional mentorship for boys across school, relationships, identity, responsibility, careers and the transition into adulthood.

HOW IT WORKS

A structured journey, not just an introduction.

01

Apply

Mentors apply and parents or guardians register boys.

02

Review

Applications are reviewed with safeguarding and suitability in mind.

03

Match

Approved participants are matched around needs, strengths and interests.

04

Grow

Mentorship continues within programme boundaries and check-ins.

REGISTER A BOY

Parent or guardian registration

Parents and legal guardians can register a boy for consideration in the Boyzclub mentorship programme.

BECOME A MENTOR

Positive role models can change a young person's direction.

Mentor applications are open for adults who are willing to support boys within Boyzclub's screening, safeguarding and programme boundaries.

SAFEGUARDING

Safety comes before matching.

Boyzclub is being designed around responsible adult screening, parent or guardian consent, clear mentor boundaries and controlled access to information about children.
