WHY IT MATTERS

Boys need safe spaces, consistent guidance and positive examples too.

We Are Boys creates intentional support around boys as they navigate school, identity, relationships, responsibility, careers and the transition into adulthood. We are not here to “fix” boys. We are here to help them be seen, guided and equipped while they grow.

HOW IT WORKS

A structured journey, not just an introduction.

01

Register & Apply

Parents or guardians register boys, and prospective male mentors apply.

02

Review & Screen

Applications are reviewed with safeguarding, suitability and programme capacity in mind.

03

Match & Orient

Approved participants are thoughtfully matched and given clear expectations and boundaries.

04

Grow & Become

Mentorship continues through structured interactions, experiences, check-ins and opportunities.
