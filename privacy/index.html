PRIVACY

Your information matters.

Boyzclub collects only the information needed to review registrations, mentor applications and partnership enquiries, support programme administration, and protect participants.

What we collect

Depending on the form you use, we may collect contact details, guardian information, limited information about a boy's age, grade, interests and mentorship needs, mentor background information, and partnership enquiry details.

Why we collect it

We use this information to review applications, assess suitability, support safeguarding, communicate with applicants and guardians, and administer the Boyzclub mentorship programme.

Information about children

Registration information about a child should be submitted by a parent, legal guardian or authorised caregiver. We aim to collect only what is reasonably necessary for programme screening and matching.

Who can access it

Submitted information is not intended to be publicly visible. Access should be limited to authorised Boyzclub programme administrators and people who need the information for safeguarding or programme administration.

How long we keep it

Information should be kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the programme, safeguarding, legal or administrative purposes, after which it should be securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

Your choices

You may ask Boyzclub to correct or remove information where appropriate. Formal contact details and a complete POPIA-compliant privacy process should be added before the programme is opened broadly to the public.

Important: This page is an operational draft and should receive a South African privacy/POPIA review before full programme launch.
